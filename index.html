Swapnil Bhole

Real-time systems engineer: 5G PHY, FPGA/SoC, embedded & GPU

I build and debug systems where hardware, firmware and software meet under hard latency limits. I am the Software Lead for the OpenExo team at the University of Michigan, where I lead the data-collection software for a wearable exoskeleton research platform.

Before that I spent six years at C-DOT, India’s national telecom R&D lab, building a 5G O-RU PHY testbed on Zynq-7000 FPGA/ARM and running 3GPP LTE conformance and RF/PHY validation. I hold an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Michigan (2026), with coursework in computer architecture, GPU programming, embedded systems and wireless communication.

I’m interested in roles in real-time and embedded systems, 5G/wireless PHY, FPGA/SoC, and GPU or performance engineering.
